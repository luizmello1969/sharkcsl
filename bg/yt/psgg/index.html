What changes "below the belt" after 40

It's not in your head. After 40, several things start working differently — and most men were never told this could happen.

  • Blood flow to sensitive areas naturally decreases — the #1 factor behind firmness and perceived size.
  • Testosterone drops roughly 1–2% per year after 30, affecting drive, recovery and confidence.
  • Vascular health in the manhood area is a mirror of your heart health — what helps one, helps the other.
  • Lifestyle (sleep, food, circulation) plays a far bigger role than most men 40+ realize.
